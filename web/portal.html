Add firewall

Portitor logs in to the firewall over SSH once to read its address and certificate fingerprint. The credentials are not kept.

Add HA secondary

Install the secondary from the ISO (role secondary) first. Portitor logs in over SSH to the new host and to the primary, hands the secondary's details from one to the other and commits the pair: nothing is copied by hand. The credentials are not kept.
